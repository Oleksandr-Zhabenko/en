(1 Corinthians X, 23-28)
Here I will note that the identical usage of The earth is the Lord's, and the fulness thereof twice in opposite cases signifies the following. When the apostle says that one may eat everything, then these words mean that since everything is the Lord's, and the faithful are also the Lord's, they may eat everything (freedom to act). But when the apostle says that for the sake of other people's conscience it is better not to eat food offered to idols, then these words mean that since everything is the Lord's, and the faithful are also the Lord's, they can eat something else instead (freedom not to act, to act otherwise, etc.). Overall, a manifestation of freedom in a very broad sense is visible.
