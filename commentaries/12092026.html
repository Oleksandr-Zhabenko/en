Galatians V, 4 — 'ἀπὸ Χριστοῦ' — 'apo CHristou' — from Christ; without Christ. The preposition 'apo' emphasizes complete distance, separation, leaving Christ here, which is why according to sense it is often translated as without Christ. 'ἐν νόμῳ' - 'en nomo' — in the Law; by the Law. Both translation options are possible.

Galatians V, 5 — 'ἐκ πίστεως' — 'ek pisteos' — by faith / out of faith. Indicates faith as the beginning of justification, from where it starts, what is the beginning of justification.
