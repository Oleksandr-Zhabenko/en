Liturgy:
(Ephesians V, 20-26)
Ephesians V, 20 — 'ἐν ὀνόματι τοῦ κυρίου ἡμῶν Ἰησοῦ Χριστοῦ' — 'en onomati tou kyriou hemon Iesou CHristou' — in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ.

Ephesians V, 21 — 'ἐν φόβῳ Χριστοῦ' — 'en phobo CHristou' — in the fear of Christ. Fear of Christ is the same as fear of God, about which there is more at the link:
https://www.facebook.com/groups/1539152859716654/?multi_permalinks=3554517061513547

Ephesians V, 24 — 'ἐν παντί' — 'en panti' — in everything.

Ephesians V, 26 — 'ἐν ῥήματι' — 'en rhemati' — in the word; in the matter; with a word; by a matter. A polysemous word and overall phrase, but it certainly does not refer solely to words, but to the essence of the matter.

Fear of Christ — the fear through sin of grieving God, grieving Jesus Christ. More on the fear of God is finely written at the link:
https://oleksandr-zhabenko.github.io/en/DialogueOnWordsChristFear.html

The Apostle Paul prompts giving thanks to God.

Further Paul writes about love among Christians. It includes humility, a true vision of oneself. And right here he writes about the life of married spouses. Marriage in the Lord is a Mystery, a Sacrament, carrying a profound meaning, and Paul here likens it to the union of Christ and the Church. Also right here is a continuation regarding mutual humility. Many people pay attention to the fact that a wife ought to be humble regarding her husband. But not everyone pays attention to the fact that a husband, if he is like Christ, must give himself sacrificially for the sake of his wife, just as Christ gave Himself up for the salvation of the Church unto crucifixion and death on the cross. The husband is thus called to no less humility than the wife by this comparison. That is, one must understand that Paul speaks of the mutual humility of spouses, of mutual consent, of harmony and mutual understanding. (Luke III, 23-IV, 1)
Luke IV, 1 — 'ἀπὸ τοῦ Ἰορδάνου' — 'apo tou Iordanou' — from Jordan. The preposition 'apo' indicates here that Jesus completely left the lands near the Jordan. 'ἐν τῷ πνεύματι' - 'en to pneumati' — in the Spirit / by the Spirit. In Whom. 'ἐν τῇ ἐρήμῳ' - 'en te eremo' — in the wilderness. Where.
